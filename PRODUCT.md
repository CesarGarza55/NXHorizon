# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

People who want to organize and launch Nintendo Switch game ports compiled for iOS with NX2Apple on their iPhone.

## Product Purpose

NX Horizon is a launcher for a personal library of Nintendo Switch game ports on iPhone. It helps users browse their installed ports and open them through LiveContainer.

## Positioning

The launcher combines a game-focused library with LiveContainer integration, including optional direct boot setup for linked games.

## Operating Context

Users browse and manage their library on an iPhone, in portrait or landscape, then launch a selected game. They may also connect a game controller and link a LiveContainer folder.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app supports portrait and landscape layouts and lets users select automatic, portrait, or landscape orientation.
- Games can be imported, searched, filtered, favorited, viewed, and launched.
- Launch URLs can open games through LiveContainer; an optional direct boot setting configures linked games.
- The app includes controller status and deadzone settings.
- Spanish and English are supported.
- The requested experience is mobile-friendly in portrait and console-inspired in landscape.
- The app is an Expo / React Native project targeting iOS; native integrations are implemented as local Expo modules.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: NX Horizon.
- The user wants a landscape experience reminiscent of a Nintendo Switch, while retaining a comfortable mobile layout in portrait.

## Product Principles

- Keep the game library and launch action easy to reach.
- Adapt the layout to the device orientation rather than forcing landscape.
- Keep library management and device integration available without obscuring the games.
- Preserve controller and touch interaction.
